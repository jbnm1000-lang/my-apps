💡 How to Use This Guide
Navigating local Korean menus can be intimidating. We designed this guide to help you explore authentic flavors safely.
  • Select a Category: Browse through 8 distinct food categories below based on your current craving.
  • Food Roulette: Cannot decide? Tap the Roulette button for a delicious surprise recommendation.
  • Dietary Tags: Every dish includes visual tags indicating spice levels, meat content, and vegan options.
  • How to Order: Show the generated Korean phrase to the restaurant staff for effortless ordering.
🥢 Local Dining Pro Tips
  • Side dishes (Banchan) are always free and can be refilled simply by asking the staff politely.
  • In many casual restaurants, you pay at the front counter when you are ready to leave. Tipping is not required in Korea.
  • Do not stick your chopsticks vertically into a bowl of rice; it is considered bad luck.
⚠️ Important Food Safety & Allergy Notice
The dietary tags (such as Vegan, Pork-Free, or Spicy levels) provided in this application are based on general, traditional Korean recipes. However, ingredients, cooking methods, sauces, and broths can vary significantly depending on the specific restaurant, chef, or regional style. Cross-contamination may also easily occur in busy local kitchens.

This application serves strictly as a helpful general guide and CANNOT guarantee that any dish is 100% free of specific allergens or ingredients. If you have severe food allergies, strict dietary restrictions, or religious food requirements, you MUST confirm the ingredients directly with the restaurant staff before ordering and consuming.
