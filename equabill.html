✨ What is EquaBill?
Ever had a great meal ruined by the awkward math at the end? When someone orders drinks and others don't, splitting the bill evenly isn't fair. EquaBill is your ultimate smart split companion. It instantly calculates exact individual shares while distributing taxes and tips perfectly based on what each person actually consumed.
📖 How to Use
1. Bill Details: Enter the pure food cost (Subtotal), Tax, and your desired Tip Percentage from your receipt.
2. Split Rules: Enter the total number of people. If anyone ordered alcohol, enter how many people drank and the total cost of those drinks.
3. Calculate: Tap the button to generate a fair, digital receipt for everyone.
💡 Pro Tips
The 'Drinkers' feature isn't just for alcohol! If one person ordered an expensive steak or a lavish dessert while others had a light meal, simply input that premium item's price into the Total Drink Cost field. EquaBill will cleanly separate the luxury cost and apply the correct proportional tax and tip to that specific person.
⚠️ Disclaimer
This app is provided as a convenient utility tool to simplify complex bill splitting. Depending on local state tax laws, restaurant rounding policies, or post-tax tipping rules, the final calculated amount may vary by 1 or 2 cents compared to the merchant's physical receipt. Please use these results as a helpful reference for your group.
