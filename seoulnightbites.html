👋 Welcome to Korean Delivery Culture!
Korea truly never sleeps, and neither does our food. You can get a hot, fresh meal delivered to your hotel room, or even a park bench along the Han River, at 2 AM. However, navigating the local apps and knowing exactly what to order can be tricky for travelers.
  • The Apps: Baemin and Yogiyo are the biggest apps, but usually require a Korean phone number and local credit card for verification.
  • The Solution: Use our SOS Card inside to ask your hotel front desk to order for you with cash, OR use Shuttle which accepts foreign credit cards, PayPal, and Apple Pay.
  • Disclaimer: Food prices and side dishes may vary slightly by restaurant. If you have severe allergies, always double-check using a translation app, as Korean kitchens often share equipment. This app is for informational purposes.
