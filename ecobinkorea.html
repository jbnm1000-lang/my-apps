📗 How to Use & Disclaimer
Korea has one of the strictest waste management systems in the world. Sorting trash incorrectly is not just frowned upon—it is illegal.
1. Buy the Official Bags: You MUST buy specific garbage bags (Jongnyangje) from local convenience stores. Regular plastic bags are illegal for disposal.
2. Choose Category: Tap an icon below to find out exactly how to dispose of everyday items.

🚨 Fine Warning (과태료 주의)
Throwing food waste into a general bag, or throwing general trash into a recycling bin will result in a minimum fine of 100,000 KRW (approx. $80 USD). CCTV cameras and local authorities actively monitor waste disposal areas.
