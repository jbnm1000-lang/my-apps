💡 Smart Travel Tips
1. The T-Money Advantage: Purchasing a T-Money card is essential. Not only does it save you the hassle of buying single-journey tickets, but it also provides slight discounts on fares and allows free transfers between buses and subways within 30 minutes.
2. Refund Policy: You can easily refund the remaining balance of your T-Money card (up to 20,000 KRW) at any convenience store for a small processing fee. However, the initial cost of the blank card (usually 3,000 to 5,000 KRW) is non-refundable.
3. Tipping Culture: Remember that South Korea has a strict no-tipping culture. The price you see on the menu is the final price you pay. Enjoy your meals without worrying about extra service charges!
