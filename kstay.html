🛂 Avoid Overstay Penalties
Overstaying your visa in South Korea, even by a single day, can result in heavy fines, deportation, or a ban on future re-entry. K-Stay Vault calculates your exact legal departure deadline so you can book your return flight with peace of mind.
💡 How Korean Immigration Counts
According to the Korea Immigration Service, the day you pass through immigration and enter the country counts as Day 1 of your allowed stay. Our algorithm automatically factors this in to ensure you get the mathematically correct departure date.
⚠️ Disclaimer
This tool provides an estimated deadline for reference only. Visa rules may change depending on your nationality and agreements. Always verify your official expiration date with the Korea Immigration Service (Hi Korea) or your local embassy.
