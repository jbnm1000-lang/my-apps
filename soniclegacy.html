App Guide & Pro Tips
How It Works
Welcome to your ultimate musical time machine. Sonic Legacy is engineered to break you out of your repetitive listening habits. Simply select your preferred decade from the dropdown menu, and our advanced curation algorithm will instantly dive into our massive database to retrieve a legendary masterpiece.
Maximize Your Experience
1. The 'Surprise Me' Feature: Don't limit yourself to the eras you already know. The most thrilling discoveries often happen when you select 'Surprise Me (All Eras)'. You might stumble upon a 1960s soul track or a 1980s synth-pop anthem that perfectly matches your current mood.
2. Build Your Own Library: When you discover a track that gives you goosebumps, use the provided YouTube or Google search buttons immediately. Add these hidden gems to your personal Spotify or Apple Music playlists to build a truly unique music library.
Legal & Copyright Disclaimer

We do not host, store, stream, or distribute any copyrighted audio or video files within this application.
