🛏️ Navigate Korean Rooms
Korean accommodations often feature advanced appliances like floor heating (Ondol), complex washing machines, and strict recycling rules that can be confusing for travelers.
This guide helps you decode Korean buttons on appliances and provides ready-to-use Korean messages to communicate smoothly with your host or hotel front desk.
💡 Survival Tips
  • Match the Words: Compare the Korean keywords in our guide with the buttons on your remote or appliance.
  • Show or Copy: If you need help, show the generated Korean text card to the front desk, or copy it and send it to your Airbnb host.
  • Follow House Rules: Always prioritize your specific host's house manual regarding trash and appliances.
