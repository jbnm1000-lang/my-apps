💡 Golden Tips for Travelers
To make your trip truly seamless, here are a few gentle reminders that many travelers find incredibly helpful:
  • Maps: Google Maps often does not provide walking directions here. For the best experience, we highly recommend downloading Naver Map or KakaoMap before you arrive.
  • Transit Card: Getting a 'T-Money' card at the airport convenience store will make your life so much easier. You can tap it on subways, buses, and even use it in taxis. Just remember, it can only be recharged with physical cash!
  • Translation: The Papago app is wonderfully accurate for translating Korean signs or communicating with lovely locals who might not speak English perfectly.
