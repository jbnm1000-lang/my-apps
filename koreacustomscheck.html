📱 How to Use This Guide
Welcome to Korea Customs Check. To ensure a smooth and stress-free entry into South Korea, simply enter the details of the items you have purchased abroad or at duty-free shops. Our system will analyze your shopping list against the standard reference limits to help you understand if you need to prepare a customs declaration form upon arrival.
💡 Smart Travel Allowances
General Goods: Travelers are generally allowed up to USD 800 in total purchases without declaring.
Alcoholic Beverages: The allowance is up to 2 bottles, provided the total volume is under 2 Liters AND the total value is under USD 400.
Premium Perfume: You may bring up to 100ml in total.
Tobacco Products: The standard limit is 200 sticks, which is typically equivalent to one carton of cigarettes.
⚖️ Legal Notice & Disclaimer
This application serves as a general reference tool to assist with your travel preparations. Customs regulations and reference limits are subject to change and may vary depending on the traveler's age, item category, and specific purchase conditions. The results provided here do not guarantee duty-free entry. For the most accurate and up-to-date information, always refer directly to the official guidelines provided by the Korea Customs Service.
