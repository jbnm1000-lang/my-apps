✨ Dine Safely in South Korea
Welcome to your ultimate dining concierge! Korean cuisine is globally celebrated for its rich flavors, fermented sauces, and unique cooking methods. However, navigating a local Korean menu can be incredibly challenging if you have specific dietary restrictions, severe allergies, or religious food requirements.
We designed this premium, fully offline guide to help you decode hidden ingredients. Whether you need to avoid meat broths, uncover hidden shellfish in Kimchi, or find Halal-friendly options, this app acts as your personal translator. Simply select your category and show the generated Korean card directly to the restaurant staff.
💡 Golden Tips for Ordering
  • Show the Translation Card: Do not rely on English explanations. Select your diet below and show the bright Korean translation card directly to the waiter before ordering.
  • Beware of Hidden Broths: Many seemingly vegetarian Korean dishes (like stews or cold noodles) use beef or anchovy broth as a base. Always confirm using our cards.
  • Kimchi Caution: Traditional Korean Kimchi almost always contains fermented shrimp or fish sauce (Jeotgal), which is critical information for vegans and those with seafood allergies.
