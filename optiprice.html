🛒 Avoid The "Bulk Trap"
Supermarkets often trick shoppers into buying "Family Size" or "Bulk" packages by assuming bigger is always cheaper. In reality, the smaller package is frequently the better deal. OptiPrice cuts through the marketing illusions to reveal the true cost per basic unit.
💡 How to Use & Pro Tips
1. Enter Details: Input the price and size printed on the front of the packages.
2. Compare Anything: Do not worry about matching units. You can confidently compare a 16 oz box against a 1.5 lb bag. OptiPrice handles all complex unit math behind the scenes.
3. Use the Discount Detector: Is the item on sale for "20% off"? Enter that in the Discount field. OptiPrice will automatically reduce the final price before calculating the true unit value, showing you if the sale is actually worth it.
⚠️ Disclaimer
OptiPrice provides calculated value estimates based on the information provided to assist your shopping decisions. Retailers may change final pricing or apply special club discounts at the register. Always verify the final checkout amount on your store receipt.
