🚕 Ride Safely in South Korea
Korean taxis and public transit are highly efficient, but language barriers can cause serious misunderstandings. Many local taxi drivers may have difficulty reading small English text on your map app, and explaining complex requests can be stressful.
We created this reliable concierge to act as your digital flashcard. Choose your destination or request, and the app will generate a massive, high-contrast Korean text card. Just show your screen to the driver or staff for a seamless journey!
💡 Essential Transit Tips
  • Show the Large Card: Do not just point at your phone. Tap a request below and show the giant Korean text directly to the driver.
  • Always Use the Meter: Refuse taxis that try to negotiate a fixed price before starting. Our cards will help you insist on the meter.
  • T-Money is Key: T-Money cards work for subways, buses, and 99% of taxis. You can recharge them at any convenience store.
