📖 How to Use K-Signature
Welcome! K-Signature is a premium tool designed to help you discover a beautiful, deeply meaningful Korean name that perfectly matches your personal energy.
Step 1: Simply select your preferred identity from the first dropdown menu.

Step 2: Choose the unique vibe or hidden meaning you want your new name to carry.

Step 3: Tap the gold button to instantly generate your authentic name and a beautifully crafted traditional red stamp (Doojang).
💡 Pro Tips for Your New Name
1. Social Media Ready: Take a screenshot of your beautiful red stamp and use it as a unique profile picture on Instagram or TikTok.
2. Making Friends: Introduce yourself with your new Korean name at language exchange meetups or when making Korean friends. It is a fantastic icebreaker!

⚖️ Precautions & Disclaimer
