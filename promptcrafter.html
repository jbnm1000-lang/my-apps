💡 Pro Tips for Best Results
1. Use English for Logic: Even if you want the final result in your native language, generating the prompt logic in English often yields much smarter and more accurate results from Large Language Models.
2. Be Specific: In the 'Constraints' box, do not hesitate to be demanding. Tell the AI exactly how many words to write or what specific details to avoid. The more specific you are, the better the output.
3. Experiment: Try changing the 'Tone of Voice' or 'Target Audience' for the same topic. You will be amazed at how differently the AI approaches the subject.
