💡 Golden Tips for Korean Shopping
Keep these local secrets in mind to ensure a successful and enjoyable shopping experience:
  • The Reality of 'Free Size': You will frequently see "Free Size" (프리사이즈) in boutiques. This is meant to be a "one size fits most," generally accommodating US sizes 4 to 6. Always ask to try it on!
  • Shoe Sizes are Millimeters: Don't panic when you see size "240". Korean shoe sizes are simply the exact length of your foot in millimeters.
  • Fitted Silhouettes: Korean menswear and womenswear tend to have a slimmer, more tailored fit compared to Western brands. When in doubt, it is highly recommended to size up.
